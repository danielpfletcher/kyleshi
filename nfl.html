How to read this. Win chance comes from one of three places, labeled on each ticket. Market: current sportsbook prices with the book's margin removed. Pace: a projection from season stats or record so far. Estimate: a judgment call where no current price was found.

Expected gain or loss on a ticket is win chance × profit, minus lose chance × stake. Stake and odds are the wager and price from the picks sheet. The LSU each-way ticket is shown as its two halves: one pays at full odds if LSU wins the title, the other at half odds if LSU reaches the final. The outcome spread simulates the season 20,000 times and treats tickets as independent, so related tickets (the two A.J. Brown overs, the two LSU halves, the AFC West bets) make the real spread somewhat wider.
