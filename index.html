How to read this. Win chance comes from one of three places, labeled on each ticket. Market: current sportsbook prices with the book's margin removed. Pace: a projection from season stats or record so far. Estimate: a judgment call where no current price was found.

Expected gain or loss on a ticket is win chance × profit, minus lose chance × stake. Stake is the "Locked" amount and odds are the "Best Line" from the group sheet. The outcome spread simulates the season 20,000 times and treats tickets as independent, so the two Steelers unders make the real spread slightly wider.
